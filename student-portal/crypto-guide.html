What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is digital money that moves directly between people over the internet, without a bank in the middle. Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, and similar coins are recorded on a public, shared ledger (a "blockchain") instead of a private bank database — which is what makes them work the same way everywhere in the world, 24 hours a day.

Why it's great for international payments

  • Speed: most crypto payments confirm in minutes, not the 1–5 business days a bank wire can take.
  • No borders: send or receive money from almost any country without currency-exchange counters or international transfer fees.
  • Always open: crypto networks run 24/7/365.
  • Lower fees: especially for cross-border payments, network fees are often a fraction of traditional wire-transfer or card-processing fees.

How to buy crypto — step by step

1
Choose an exchange. Pick a reputable platform (Binance, Coinbase, or a P2P marketplace) that supports your country and preferred payment method.
2
Create an account and verify your ID. Exchanges require basic identity verification (KYC) — usually a photo ID and a selfie.
3
Add a payment method. Link your bank account, debit/credit card, or mobile money account.
4
Buy your coin. Choose the amount and the coin (e.g. USDT, BTC) — it lands in your exchange wallet almost instantly.
5
Send it to the address on our checkout page. Copy the wallet address shown when you choose Crypto at checkout, paste it into your exchange's "Withdraw" screen, and confirm.
Always double-check you're sending the same coin and network shown on checkout (e.g. USDT on the TRC-20 network) — sending the wrong network can cause funds to be lost.
