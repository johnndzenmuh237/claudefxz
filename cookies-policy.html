1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function and remember your preferences.

2. Cookies we use

  • Preference cookies: remember settings like dark/light theme.

3. Managing cookies

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner shown on your first visit, and change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Most browsers also let you block cookies globally in their settings, though this may affect site functionality.
